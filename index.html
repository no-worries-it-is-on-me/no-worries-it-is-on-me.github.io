Questions

Things people ask us.

Will it replace my receptionist?

No. It covers the hours nobody is at the desk and the questions nobody should have to answer twenty times a day. Your team gets fewer interruptions and better-prepared customers.

What if it doesn't know the answer?

It says so, takes the customer's details and passes the conversation to you. It never makes up a price, a slot or a diagnosis.

Will customers know they're talking to a bot?

Yes — it introduces itself as your assistant. People don't mind a bot; they mind waiting until morning for “yes, we're open Saturday”.

I'm not a clinic or a salon. Does it still work?

If customers message you with questions before they buy or book, it works. Clinics and salons are just where the 11pm messages pile up fastest.

How long does setup really take?

A few minutes to a working bot from your website. If you want bookings, reminders or a row in your spreadsheet, tell us and we build it in at no extra cost.
